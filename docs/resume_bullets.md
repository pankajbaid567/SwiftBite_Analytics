# 📝 SwiftBite Analytics — Resume Bullet Points

## Bullet 1: End-to-End SQL Analytics
> Designed and implemented a **relational database** with 8 normalized tables and **27,000+ rows** of synthetic data to simulate a food delivery platform's analytics warehouse, enabling complex multi-table analysis across customers, orders, restaurants, and delivery operations.

## Bullet 2: Customer Segmentation & Retention
> Built **RFM customer segmentation** using **CTEs and NTILE window functions** in PostgreSQL, identifying that top 20% of customers contribute 46% of revenue; proposed a VIP loyalty program to improve high-value customer retention.

## Bullet 3: Cohort & Retention Analysis
> Performed **cohort analysis and 30-day retention modeling** using **self-joins, window functions (LAG/LEAD), and date arithmetic**, discovering a 30.8% 30-day retention rate and identifying behavioral patterns that differentiate retained vs churned users.

## Bullet 4: Revenue & Funnel Optimization
> Analyzed **month-over-month revenue trends** and **order funnel conversion** using **running totals, RANK(), and CASE WHEN** aggregations across 5,000+ orders, uncovering an 11% cancellation rate with individual restaurants reaching 31%.

## Bullet 5: Operational Efficiency
> Evaluated **delivery partner performance** across 10 cities using **DENSE_RANK() and multi-metric scoring**, and identified that premium members order at 2.2x the frequency of standard users — informing fleet allocation and conversion strategy.
